# Compliance Map submission draft

## What it does

Compliance Map takes one activity and at least three places, researches official government rules, and returns a consistent side-by-side comparison of registrations, licences, filings, fees, processing times, renewals and ongoing obligations. It includes source links and checked dates, distinguishes conditional rules from confirmed obligations, and flags missing evidence.

## TinyResearch use

TinyResearch performs the central cross-jurisdiction investigation. TinySearch discovers authorities, then TinyResearch reads and synthesizes each place's rules into the same six categories. TinyFetch independently retrieves the cited government pages, checks quoted passages, stamps the actual retrieval date and hashes the content. Unsupported numbers are excluded. A completed research run, original report and source receipts are retained.

## Demonstration activities

1. Open a small café in Westminster, Manchester or Edinburgh.
2. Register a single member LLC operating in Wyoming, Delaware or California.

Use the two saved live examples, then run a new activity or change the places to see the same research pipeline handle a new input. Saved examples are dated snapshots, not disguised new web checks.

## Why the decision is useful

The comparison makes the difference between a known fee subtotal and an actual total explicit, separates one-time fees from recurring taxes, and refuses to fabricate an overall approval duration. Its recommendation explains the evidence and unresolved trade-offs. Incomplete data produces a provisional investigation order instead of a false easiest-location claim.
